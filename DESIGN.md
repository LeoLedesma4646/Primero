## Brand & Style

The design system embodies "Energía que se siente bien"—a balanced, plant-powered ethos that bridges clean wellness with vibrant, everyday youth culture. Designed for active urban consumers seeking clean functional vitality without synthetic jitter or crash, the visual style combines modern organic minimalism with earthy editorial warmth.

The aesthetic pairs breathable, warm cream foundations with deep botanical greens and sun-warmed citrus accents. Rather than sterile pharmaceutical wellness or hyper-aggressive energy drink styling, this system feels approachable, nutritious, wholesome, and active. Soft fluid geometry, high-touch tactile surfaces, natural photography, and structured benefit badges establish an aura of trust, transparency, and refreshing vitality.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from botanical and earthy elements:

- **Deep Forest Green (`#1E3A2B`)**: The primary brand anchor. Used for primary interactive actions, high-impact headings, active navigation states, and grounding packaging containers.
- **Warm Citrus Amber (`#D96B27`)**: The active secondary color representing positive vitality and natural alertness. Used for primary highlight badges, promotional ribbons, key calls-to-action, and the Café + Naranja flavor profile.
- **Matcha Olive (`#7E9A4B`)**: The tertiary botanical hue representing natural hydration, herbal infusion, and sustainable lifestyle elements. Utilized for organic benefit checkmarks, flavor tags (Té verde-Limón-Menta), and secondary visual indicators.
- **Toasted Amber Brown (`#935A34`)**: Auxiliary earth tone representing roots, coffee bean infusions, and grounding craft paper textures.
- **Soft Cream Canvas (`#F5F1E9`)**: The dominant background foundation. Replaces harsh digital white with an unbleached, natural parchment feel. Elevated containers and cards use pure off-white (`#FBF9F5`) to establish gentle tonal separation.
- **Text & Contrast Structure**: Body text utilizes `#242824` on cream layers for optimal WCAG AAA readability, preserving contrast without introducing cold, synthetic pure blacks.

## Typography

The type system blends structural geometric confidence with human warmth. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Outfit`)**: Outfit's rounded geometric construction communicates approachable authority, modern wellness, and vigor. Display headers can embrace all-caps lockups with subtle letter-spacing for brand and flavor announcements, matching retail packaging hierarchy.
- **Body & Functional UI (`DM Sans`)**: Balanced, readable, and low-contrast, DM Sans provides clear, unpretentious legibility for ingredient lists, nutritional panels, and editorial product stories.
- **Benefit Badges & Microcopy**: Badges adopt uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.06em`) with medium-to-bold weights to mimic stamped botanical labels ("BAJO EN AZÚCAR", "SIN TAURINA", "ENERGÍA LIMPIA").

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid 12-column grid on desktop screens, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile viewports.

- **Rhythm & Whitespace**: Generous section spacing (`space-2xl`) mirrors the organic, breathable nature of the product. Content modules are never crowded; cards and informational panels rely on interior padding of `space-md` to `space-lg` to create clear breathing room.
- **Card Clustering**: Grids arrange flavor showcases and nutritional benefit clusters into balanced 3-column rows on desktop, reflowing to horizontally scrollable carousels or single-column stacked lists on mobile viewports.
- **Edge Restraint**: Outer boundaries retain consistent canvas margins (`margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `margin: 3rem`) maintaining alignment across photography heroes, split content blocks, and retail partner sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through organic tactile layering rather than synthetic multi-tier drop shadows:

- **Tonal Layering**: Depth is achieved primarily by placing light organic surfaces (`#FBF9F5` or pure translucent whites) against the `#F5F1E9` cream base.
- **Diffused Earth Shadows**: When components require elevation (e.g., sticky product purchase bars, floating modal dialogs, interactive flavor cards on hover), shadows are warm-tinted and deeply diffused:
  - `elevation-sm`: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 58, 43, 0.06)`
  - `elevation-md`: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(30, 58, 43, 0.08)`
  - `elevation-lg`: `0 20px 36px -8px rgba(30, 58, 43, 0.12)`
- **Low-Contrast Organic Borders**: Unselected cards and line items employ a delicate 1px boundary (`rgba(30, 58, 43, 0.08)`) that defines geometry cleanly without introducing visual noise.

## Shapes

The shape system leverages organic, friendly curves that reflect pebble and leaf geometries:

- **Standard Containers & Cards**: Set to `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) up to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for cards, dialogs, and modular packaging blocks.
- **Interactive Badges & Pills**: Tags, functional indicators, flavor chips, and call-to-action buttons use full pill rounding (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to convey softness, safety, and natural touch.
- **Visual Cutouts & Asymmetry**: Occasional decorative accent surfaces mimic the craft package die-cut window with generous corner radiuses (`1.5rem` to `2rem`) to emphasize retail continuity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid Forest Green (`#1E3A2B`) background with light cream text (`#F5F1E9`). On hover, smoothly transitions to `#2A4F3B` with subtle scale lift.
- **Secondary Action**: Pill-shaped, warm Citrus Amber (`#D96B27`) background with white text for promotional checkouts or direct "Probar Ahora" conversions.
- **Outline Action**: Transparent base with 1.5px solid `#1E3A2B` border, dark green text, transitioning to `#1E3A2B` at 6% fill on hover.

### Benefit Badges & Chips
- **Functional Benefit Badges**: Pill-shaped badges featuring minimal line-art botanical icons (e.g., leaf for natural ingredients, lightning for clean energy, droplet for low sugar). Outlined in 1px subtle green (`rgba(30, 58, 43, 0.15)`) with matcha green or forest green text and light cream interior.
- **Flavor Indicator Tags**: Small pill chips with flavor-specific fills (e.g., matcha green tint for Té Verde, amber tint for Café Naranja, golden yellow tint for Maracuyá).

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Product Pack Showcase**: Surface `#FBF9F5` container with `rounded-lg` corners, 1px perimeter border (`rgba(30, 58, 43, 0.08)`), featuring centered vertical 250ml can imagery, flavor typography, benefit icon row, and an integrated bottom action CTA.
- **Lifestyle & Education Cards**: Split-layout cards pairing full-bleed editorial imagery of active young urbanites with warm cream text panels explaining clean sustained energy.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Inputs**: Rounded-md (`0.5rem`) inputs with `#FBF9F5` fill, 1px border (`rgba(30, 58, 43, 0.15)`), shifting to a focused 1.5px Forest Green ring (`#1E3A2B`) with a warm glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Fully circular radio controls and soft-rounded checkboxes (`rounded-sm`). When checked, filled with Forest Green (`#1E3A2B`) carrying a white checkmark.

### Packs & Flavor Switcher
- **Pack Selector Segment**: Connected segmented control pill with Forest Green active states and cream inactive segments, providing instantaneous pricing and pack configuration toggles (Pack x3, Pack x6, Pack Mixto).
